## Contrarian hook

Everyone is rushing to inject AI into their organisation. New tools, new pipelines, new copilots. The urgency is real and I understand it.

But here is what I keep seeing: teams trying to embed AI into a foundation that was already cracked.

If your digital vision is fuzzy, your teams cannot agree on what to use AI for. If your operating model runs on approval chains, AI-generated code still queues in the same bottlenecks. If knowledge is hoarded, the people who understand the tools and the people who understand the business rarely talk.

AI does not fix a broken engineering culture. It amplifies it.

Before you invest in AI capability, ask yourself: how does our organisation score across the eight dimensions of the Engineering Culture Model? Clear Digital Vision, Empowering Operating Model, Knowledge Driven, Epic Workplace, and four more.

You probably have two or three dimensions getting all the attention. That is exactly where the drag is hiding.

## Story format

A few years ago a good friend and I spent a day walking through a city. No agenda, no client to prep for, just walking and pushing back on each other.

At some point we asked: what do we actually do? The first answers were all technology. Cloud migrations, DevOps, platform engineering. True, but it felt like the list of things we carry, not the reason we carry them.

After a few hours we landed somewhere different. Every person on the team, developer, architect, agile coach, transformation consultant, is working toward the same thing: helping organisations become IT companies. And if you want to be an IT company, you need to behave like one.

That conversation was the seed of the Engineering Culture Model. Eight dimensions. Not a checklist, not a maturity ladder. A diagnostic for seeing where an organisation is strong and where it is quietly breaking down.

With AI changing everything right now, that diagnostic has never been more relevant.

## Question format

How does your organisation actually score on engineering culture?

Not the answer you give in a board presentation. The honest one.

Most organisations I work with have two or three dimensions absorbing all the attention, usually delivery speed and software quality, while the other five drift. That drift is almost always behind the stalling, the attrition, and the missed delivery promises.

The Engineering Culture Model maps eight dimensions: from how clear your digital vision really is, to whether this is an environment where good people genuinely want to work. Plot an honest read across all eight and the gaps become visible in a way that no project dashboard ever shows.

With the current AI wave, those gaps matter more than ever. Injecting AI into a cracked foundation does not accelerate you. It accelerates the cracks.

Where is your organisation quietly breaking down?
